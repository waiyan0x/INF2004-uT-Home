Match the physical boards to the drawing first

Match your photo: Board A is on the left, with a–j across the top. Board B is on the right, with J–A across the top and A–J along the bottom. On Board B, always use the bottom letters and right-hand row numbers. Its two topmost rows sit above the printed 0 and remain unused. The ESP's USB socket points up on Board A.

A · j30 means Board A, column j, row 30. B · a14 means Board B's bottom A column and right-hand row 14 (the left edge shows 46 at that height). A · R−23 means Board A, right blue rail, the 23rd hole counted from the top. Rail numbers are guide labels, not printed row numbers. Each board has 63 main rows and 50 holes per rail.

Five holes a–e in one numbered row connect together; f–j connect separately. Power rails run lengthwise and may split in the middle. The plan bridges holes 25 and 26 on each used rail. Verify actual continuity with USB unplugged.

Wire colours in this guide indicate purpose, not the colours of your existing cables. Module pin labels are schematic and do not establish connector order. Follow the module's printed labels. Crossing wires do not connect unless they share an endpoint.
