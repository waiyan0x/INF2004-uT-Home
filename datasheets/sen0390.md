# SEN0390 peripheral description

Supplied 16-page SEN0390 PDF; no clear document revision was identified.

**Reference only: compilation is blocked for this device.**

Pages 1 and 13 give write/read address bytes 0x94/0x95. Removing the R/W bit
yields seven-bit address 0x4A. Page 1 also identifies EN as active high.
Page 5 places the 32-bit light data in registers 0x00..0x03, suggesting a
one-byte register pointer and four response bytes for a future reviewed read.

However, p. 14 describes a different consecutive register pair, while the read
diagrams on p. 15 explicitly mark a delay greater than 10 ms after the read
address acknowledgement and before data. The current transaction rule provides
no such delay. Do not reinterpret that requirement as an ordinary immediate
register read or silently borrow the historical driver as proof of compliance.
Resolve the register/timing ambiguity and EN control before enabling this entry.
